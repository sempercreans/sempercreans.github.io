## 4.2. Character Analysis Essay

### “Johann”

“Johann” is a serious, rigid, by-the-books person with a mysterious, intimidating, and cold aura. He carries himself with the tact of a wise cunning man, usually speaking in analogies or metaphors and very blunt.  He looks like he has it all as the perfect Marleyan, Married to one of the most prosperous wine families of the mainland with his beautiful late wife, Madonna, and daughter Magdalena, a loving husband and father, handsome, rich, intelligent,, and most importantly, A pure lineage. 

He may be adopted to a prestigious officer away from the Marleyan mainland and his true origin was of speculation back then, but his blood test and works prove he is truly a devout son of Marley. And he has this respect as an orphaned boy who made his way to the top.  His appearance even looks like he could be those gods and statues in ancient times with his tall build, strong nose, long face, and broad shoulders. He dresses  Polished, Elitist, and Clean, always seen wearing button-ups, ties, and suits similar to his tailored uniform which feels like his own skin. 

He can be as stiff as a board, which does not make him the most popular, as he can be too blunt which gives the impression that he seldom cares about the opinions of others,  However, he is not entirely reckless with his words and critiques when the situation calls for it. He only speaks when necessary, but he is intelligent and firm when he does. He has wit but is not the super snarky or scheming type of player who announces his every move,  he’s more of the monotonous boring stoic, but he is not to be messed with or manipulated. He is not oozing with magnetism, you can smell the dryness and coldness from a mile away, but he has this special charisma. As much as people fear the man, they respect him.

He is a professional with a strong sense of honor and duty. He knows people, how to read them well and get to their skin; how to break them; He can be a follower by sucking up to a boss, while still maintaining his integrity and pride, and he can be a leader by commanding a room full of rookies. He was always stressed and tired of most people, which would explain his grumpiness based on how busy the man was. For an important chunk of his life, he was a family man, and almost all of it; a distinguished military officer. Johann also is a skilled businessman and has a big part in managing one of the most successful wineries in the mainland continent that prides itself on having “the purest wine in Marley” and thus in his case the world due to its lack of subhumans working for them.
### Race and Duty

Johann’s most prominent belief is his devout patriotism for the Marleyan nation and his racism and prejudice against those who aren't, especially Eldians, who he like the rest of Marleyan society views as the lowest of the low. His job even shows that he is a high-ranking officer of the public health authorities. He is In charge of blood tests, and other services that separate Marley's cattle like immigrants and so on. Instead of being one who vows to treat anybody without discrimination, he is a doctor of eugenics and a devout follower of the racial hygiene and purity of  Marleyan society.  Doing countless experiments and eugenics of  people to study their differences and prove himself and his race superior all for the sake of his research and nation. He handles many other cases but his job specializes in children.  He is one to monitor whether or not those were born out of wedlock from “unholy unions”  and one to give out the honor and prestige of a Marleyan Blood Certificate while also ruining any chance of one's future if one is discovered to be an Eldian. The most important task to him in being Marleyan is defining who isn't, especially their enemies and those who give them a bad reputation. 

He has ripped children out of their mothers’ arms, sterilized, experimented, and euthanized, he sleeps soundly at night without guilt for his atrocities, even when he is a father himself. Because to him, the difference is that they aren't human, they are slaves or vermin, deserve to be whipped back to shape after the crimes of their ancestors, while his family is on top and the superior masters. He is so used to it that he makes little jabs occasionally but doesn't do it out of sadism or pleasure or make it a laughing matter or for the sake of evolving as some mad scientist tropes do. But to him it is out of duty for his race against the others. The scarier side to this is some small part of him struggling deep down does recognize their humanity, he sees their faces, yet that won't ever distract him from his job. What he does is methodical and to him; a mercy to take away those and future generations who are unworthy of life. 

There is no denying he hates other races or any other blemishes to society, but to him, nothing makes him more repulsed than the idea of mixing, whether through cultures or by blood, it's all the same to Johann, especially with his Marleyan heritage. He is more than fine with people being separated, even if he dislikes them. But for them to mix? To bring a child into this world whether through love or force and lust like some men do, He views the result as children of sin and shame. It is one thing to be another race to him but to taint something as pure as his blood, as immaculate as his people, it infuriates him to no end. Understandably, some people will say he does this out of hate for others, but to him as much as he does it out of spite, he does it out of love. Love for his race, his integrity and reputation, and especially his family.
### Family

Johann’s race and blood are the main core of his beliefs, but family is almost close to something he is dedicated his whole being to. A family is what makes a race; it's what builds civilizations and humanity as a whole. It brings him great pride and joy that he did his duty to his nation. His own immediate family that he made himself—he adores the idea of them more than them as people, but he has genuine love and special affection for them. 

His wife Madonna is the only one to ever really melt his coldness and as much as he looks like he's stern and doesn't stand stubborn wild men, especially as workmates, it is ironic the most precious people in his life are two wild rambunctious women who won’t take no for an answer. 


Fatherhood is extremely special to  Johann. His daughter Lena was one of the best things that happened in his life  He is a bit awkward has deep wounds from his childhood and is not the super most excellent or warm father, but he does his best to provide and be there. 

Before "the incident" he was rather warm and affectionate towards his two precious girls. He is stern but he doesn't hit or beat them even if it is common in his time and even allowed in Marleyan society. ## 4.3. Identity

### <span style="color:rgb(112, 112, 112)">4.3.1. Gender Expression and Identity</span>

Yakov is a Cisgender masculine person who uses He/Him pronouns. On the surface, He expresses himself as a rather traditional masculine man, hair often kept short, wearing pants and suits. Being a man is something that subconsciously affects his beliefs and decisions due to being the dominant sex when it comes to matters of eugenics and colonization though themes of gender are not that important to his story.

### <span style="color:rgb(120, 120, 120)">4.3.2. Sexual Orientation and Attraction Preferences</span>

Yakov would classify himself as Heterosexual, attracted only to those who are feminine presenting and cisgender as well.

 He is rather reserved when it comes to these types of topics, often flustered and disinterested when it came to the talks boys would usually have or the  drunk crude talks with his fellow men and workmates, he never pursued any ladies of the night like most of his line of work, or was that much of a player, all of his sexual experiences were in established relationships.


 Along with that like most people of his time he had homophobic view points, and was not afraid to voice them out loud. 

But Yakov is not that sexist enough to have  the stereotypical view men have of  women to  just be kind, obedient meek housewives good at cooking and cleaning and nothing else, sure they are nice for a while, but he thinks that those who like those types are simple-minded. 

Of course,  like men of his time with  his view points on race, he is patriarchal and deems it important for women to participate in their role as childrearers in society, and definitely has those desires when it comes to matters in bed *ahem*. 

But it's weird that he secretly has a thing for those who are completely opposite to him, headstrong, loud. He hates it on men and workmates who are lazy, but on women, he has a bit more of a type for them to be honest. 

He wants them to be intelligent just as they are domestic, maybe it is something related to his mother, Magdalena, who was a very  educated person. He views intelligence as competence, someone he can have witty back and forth with and talk about current events, etc. 

Most of all, Yakov is a nationalist and those are things he deeply desires in a partner, not someone who is mid-mannered or lukewarm about their  blood, he thinks people like those are free loaders to society and the empire. He wants people with the same views and passion for Marley above everything. A woman who would bear healthy strong children for his people. To raise children is a huge factor he had when it came to seeking relationships back then. 

He had a few girlfriends and experience before his wife in his college years. His first girlfriends were Augusta Mendler and Martha Yelts.  They were the sweet  soft hearted intelligent types who decided to have their shot thinking they might get rejected but Yakov seemingly said yes because he had nothing to do and wanted something new. There were kisses and times where it got a bit heated at nights, he was attracted and enjoyed them,  but they all felt shallow to him. 

Madonna was the person he would classify as his soulmate. They were complete opposites.  She was flamboyant and artistic, he was stoic and often monotonous. But when he met Madonna something awakened in him. As much as they were different, She was intelligent, a devout nationalist and blood purist, they just had different ways of showing their love for Marley. Madonna compliments him as much as they disagree and bicker, she spices some things up in many aspects of his life and brings out a softer childlike side. 

When it comes to coupling, Yakov always is the dominant partner. Though…he doesn't mind when a receptive partner takes charge as long as he will always be the penetrative one.

 But He does not really have a thing for things like Erotic humiliation though, in fact it's a big turn off for him, at the end of the day he wants to keep a hold of that masculine pride of his. 

The only roleplay he was okay with doing was anything his wife coaxed him into situations about Marley or Ancient Marley. I wont go into detail onto this haha-

### <span style="color:rgb(120, 120, 120)">4.3.4. Religious Beliefs</span>

Yakov though devout to Marley and its history would never call himself a devotee to its religions or  ever consider himself as a religious person at all unlike his wife, Madonna. He appreciates  the aesthetics and the role the gods played in the history of Marley, how they gave them hope in two millennia of enslavement and persecution and to him the fact they still stand proves his race is superior, but like the rest of Marleyan society, he is a pretty secular person.  

He never made any hard conclusions in that type of meaning of life so  he is pretty much Agnostic. Sure, he visits every once in a while in temples to celebrate Saturnalia and feasts, he wears togas on some occasions, and even had a very traditional wedding per his wife and in laws request, but he never had a meaningful connection to it that it changes his lifestyle, personality, and beliefs in anyway.

He scoffs at the rest of religions as he views them as coming from barbarian peoples.  He met a few of those hidden fringe devotees of Ymir in his job,  he heard all their ramblings and ravings, and it made torturing and interrogating them more enjoyable just as it was annoying as  he made them all to the beasts they are to him to spend it in Paradis.

The old gods of Marley are the only religious faith he can ever tolerate.  He was indifferent to it growing up. Those who brainwashed him always told about such things, but it was more on how their race was superior than anything related to the stories  and lessons of faith and hope itself.

His wife, Madonna, made him entertain the idea of religion, but never fully.  He always thought of religions as something only stupid old hags would talk about, but someone with as much intellect and charm as Madonna talking about her passions? He was intrigued. 

There were some disagreements between them but they respected each other's beliefs and in fact  grew even more interested in each other for their foreign-like views that they did not see eye to eye when they first were interested with each other. They had many philosophical talks and banter but the two greatly respected one another, and so Yakov began to warm up to the idea,  even just a little bit. He was also okay with raising their daughter in her customs, if it meant she'd be more like his beloved wife, then so be it. 

The only times Yakov ever genuinely prayed or wanted there for gods to judge him was at the birth of his firstborn, and when he lost Madonna and  almost his little Lena that one fateful night in his birthday. THe one time he felt like his world was on axis,  were when Both situations he felt truly powerless, losing his family all over again.  He is a man who likes control in every corner of his life, how people view him, so he has a distaste for religion as it dictates him what he should be and what to do. Yakov is a man of little faith, not only in matters of religious beliefs but also in humanity and existence as a whole except when it comes to matters of Marley.
