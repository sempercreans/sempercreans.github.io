Hello, Welcome to my website! This is a place where I store all my characters, art, and lore.  I aim to make this site my finished guide, and by the end, I will have all my character profiles detailing how I developed them, their character analysis, and backstories will be done, along with almost all of my various series events and introductions. I even plan for this to be a good archive for even those characters I do not focus on that much, as an “oc mom” I can never stop loving my babies.

I've been there since the great departures of 2021 and 2023, when the anime and manga ended but Attack on Titan is the heart of all of my works and where my most meaningful memories and relationships come from, past and present. It is my entire philosophy at this point!

I am a very slow artist, prone to breaks, and not particularly well-suited socially, but one thing about me is that I am NEVER a quitter. Even if some people who made it worth sharing this story in the first place will leave. Even if it won't last forever, I hope someone out there knows how much I love these creations in my head, and everything necessary was written and drawn.

I am not that accomplished or productive, as my works do not have a complete form as of yet, like books or comics, but I  put my all into finishing the stories I started. I like to think of their pages as my “books” in a way.

I am a dedicated and passionate storyteller who wants to show the broken, fallen world we live in. That we are all shades of gray, yet there's still some good even in the most wicked of souls.

In particular, I personally love themes of family, dysfunctional ones, and generational trauma, and this will be a huge theme for my works. Please be warned, though, I tackle topics like all kinds of abuse, sexual, physical or emotional in their backstories. I try my best to make everybody complex and “morally gray” as like in our real world everything was passed down, but I know where I stand and do not excuse anything.

I am also interested in topics in history about 18th-20th century Europe and heritage and culture as a whole,not just Western ones. Most of my works will handle the topics of nationalismm as most of my characters’ heritage is important to them, but overall I like to think my works are very anti-war and imperialism in spite of their aesthetics.

I would also love to find people of similar interests and those passionate about their characters and stories too. I hope you enjoy taking a look here and love my creations as much as I do!
